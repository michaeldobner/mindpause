## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

First version of BLOCKS, the sixth game of the MIND PAUSE collection.

### New
* **Block puzzle**: three pieces on the tray, full rows and columns clear at the same time, once all three are placed three new ones arrive.
* **Three modes**: Classic (8 × 8), Wide (10 × 10) and Calm (all three pieces always fit, a full board clears itself).
* **13 shapes** in 37 orientations, from a single block to a square of nine, drawn with weights.
* **Points**: 1 per cell, 20, 60, 120, 200 … for 1, 2, 3, 4 … lines, times the streak, 300 for an empty board.
* **Streak**: grows with every clear and only breaks after three pieces in a row clear nothing.
* **Dragging**: the piece floats above the finger, snaps into place, shows the lines that would clear and glides back when it does not fit.
* **Undo** (one step, also after the end and after New) and a golden **hint**.
* **Keyboard**: 1, 2, 3, arrows, Enter, Escape, H and Z.
* **Design** in the colours of SPRING and QUEEN Midnight: night blue plate with hollows, pieces of blue ceramic, black ceramic at the end.
* **Sounds** of ceramic on wood as in the whole collection.
* Best scores and stars per mode, the game is saved after every piece, playable offline, German and English.
