# Changelog

All notable changes to BLOCKS. [Deutsch](CHANGELOG.de.md)

## 1.1.1 (2026-10-05)

### Fixed
* **Levels visible after the update:** players who had played a free mode before 1.1 landed in their old saved game after the update and saw nothing of the levels. BLOCKS now starts once with level 1 and announces "New: 30 levels". A running old game is kept and comes back with **Undo**.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-05)

### New
* **30 levels** in six chapters. Every level starts with its own mirror symmetric board of black starting blocks. It is complete once all starting blocks are cleared and the points goal is reached.
* **Rising difficulty as a sawtooth:** the density of the starting board and the points goal rise within a chapter and dip slightly at the start of the next. New shapes arrive chapter by chapter, chapter 1 uses small pieces only.
* **Every level can be solved:** starting board and piece order are fixed, and the computer player has played through every level. Its number of pieces is the par for three stars.
* **Stars and progress:** ★ complete, ★★ with at most one and a half times the par, ★★★ with at most the par. A completed level unlocks the next. Progress, stars and best scores stay on the device.
* **Fair start:** in every level and every mode the first three pieces always fit.
* In a level, the line above the board shows the points goal and the number of starting blocks left.
* The picker is now called **Levels** and shows the 30 levels with a preview of their starting board, followed by the free modes. New players start with level 1.

### Changed
* Classic and Wide start with a lightly filled board (15 % of the cells), Calm with 20 %.
* At the end of a game the pieces darken instead of turning black, so they stay distinct from starting blocks.
* In a level, the hint prefers spots that clear starting blocks.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

First version of BLOCKS, the sixth game of the MIND PAUSE collection.

### New
* **Block puzzle**: three pieces on the tray, full rows and columns clear at the same time, once all three are placed three new ones arrive.
* **Three modes**: Classic (8 × 8), Wide (10 × 10) and Calm (all three pieces always fit, a full board clears itself).
* **13 shapes** in 37 orientations, from a single block to a square of nine, drawn with weights.
* **Points**: 1 per cell, 20, 60, 120, 200 … for 1, 2, 3, 4 … lines, times the streak, 300 for an empty board.
* **Streak**: grows with every clear and only breaks after three pieces in a row clear nothing.
* **Dragging**: the piece floats above the finger, snaps into place, shows the lines that would clear and glides back when it does not fit.
* **Undo** (one step, also after the end and after New) and a golden **hint**.
* **Keyboard**: 1, 2, 3, arrows, Enter, Escape, H and Z.
* **Design** in the colours of SPRING and QUEEN Midnight: night blue plate with hollows, pieces of blue ceramic, black ceramic at the end.
* **Sounds** of ceramic on wood as in the whole collection.
* Best scores and stars per mode, the game is saved after every piece, playable offline, German and English.
