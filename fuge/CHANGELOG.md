# Changelog

All notable changes to FUGE. [Deutsch](CHANGELOG.de.md)

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-04)

First version of FUGE, the fourth game of the MIND PAUSE collection.

### New
* **Falling blocks** in a 10 × 20 box, the seven classic shapes.
* **Four modes**: Classic (endless, faster every 10 lines), Sprint (40 lines against the clock), 3 minutes (score in fixed time) and Calm (slow, no game over).
* **Modern mechanics**: 7-bag randomizer, three-piece preview, hold, ghost piece, SRS rotation with wall kicks, 0.5 s lock delay with at most 15 resets.
* **Guideline scoring** with T-spins and minis, back to back, combos and all clear. Four lines at once are called a **Quart**.
* **Gestures**: drag to move cell by cell, tap to rotate (left counterclockwise, right clockwise), swipe down to drop, up to hold.
* **Keyboard** with delayed auto repeat (170 ms, then every 50 ms).
* **Pieces as workpieces** of matte lacquered wood in seven curated colours, a lacquered navy box with an ivory inlay.
* **Game feel**: soft gliding, visible rotation, a gleam on lock, trail and a small give of the box on hard drop, lines dissolving from the centre, a wave through the dot grid on level up.
* **Sounds** of wood, with the collection's ceramic tone for cleared lines.
* Pause with controls help, the game is saved when you leave and resumes paused.
* Best scores and stars per mode, settings for ghost piece and patterns on the pieces, works offline, German and English.

### Shell 1.3.0
* FUGE uses the shell's new custom buttons (Hold, Pause) and hides “Undo last move” on the result card.
